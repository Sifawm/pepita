# 10 · Routines and day planning

**Principles from the book it touches:** **habit stacking** (chains), "decisive moments", environment design and the two-minute rule for starting a routine.

## How each app handles it

| App | Model |
|---|---|
| **Routinery** | **Sequential routine with a timer per step**, a voice announcing the next step, pause, skip or adjust on the fly. Live Activity, AirPods. Day view as a list, calendar or timeline. Pomodoro, white noise. **Skipping a step doesn't break the routine.** Highly rated by people with ADHD |
| **Fabulous** | Morning, afternoon and evening routines with an alarm, checklist and step-by-step **routine mode** (Dynamic Island, PiP). The free plan only includes the morning one |
| **Productive** | Morning, afternoon and evening blocks with customizable time slots. They're the backbone of the today view |
| **Habitify** | Time of day (only organizes, doesn't alert) and areas. Stacking by event |
| **Finch** | Plan tomorrow's goals in the evening check-in. Time of day |
| **HabitNow / TickTick** | **Habits + tasks** in the same daily planner. TickTick adds a calendar and Pomodoro |
| **Strides** | Tags + *Today* list. Tasks (v20) |
| **Atoms** | Only stacking. No planner |
| **Streaks, Way of Life, Loop, Habitica** | Categories or tags. Habitica has checklists inside tasks |
| **Beeminder, stickK, HabitShare** | No |

## Market patterns

- Three approaches:
  1. **Grouping by time of day** (Productive, Fabulous, Habitify).
  2. **Guided timed sequence** (Routinery, Fabulous' routine mode).
  3. **Planner combined with tasks** (TickTick, HabitNow).
- Mixing tasks and habits appeals to people who want a single app, but dilutes the focus (TickTick's habits module is "thin").

## Gaps and opportunities

- The **routine as explicit stacking**: each step is triggered when the previous one ends, and the first one is anchored to an existing habit ("after coffee…").
- A **2-minute version of the whole routine** for bad days: just the first step or the minimum steps.
- Decide whether to get into tasks and planning. From what the market shows, **it isn't worth it in the first version**.
