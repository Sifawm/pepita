# 08 · Social, accountability and contracts

**Principles from the book it touches:** 2nd law (join a culture where your behavior is the norm) and the inverse of the 4th law (make it unsatisfying): **accountability partner** and **habit contract** with consequences and witnesses.

## How each app handles it

| App | Model | Detail |
|---|---|---|
| **stickK** | **Commitment contract** (the closest thing to the book's contract) | Goal + deadline + stake + human **referee** + **supporters**. The referee only verifies when you say you succeeded and their verdict prevails. Where the money goes: stickK or an **anti-charity** (an organization opposed to your values; stickK keeps 50%). Without a referee, the contract is "On your Honor". Journal visible to the witnesses |
| **Beeminder** | **Escalating financial commitment** | Stake $0 → 5 → 10 → 30 → 90 → 270; the first derail is free. The money goes to Beeminder (on the most expensive plan, 50% can go to a charity). No human referee: verification is your data or automatic logging. There are group goals |
| **Habitica** | **Cooperative party** | Parties with chat and boss quests: **your misses damage the whole party**. Challenges with gem prizes. Guilds and the Tavern closed in 2023 |
| **Finch** | **Gentle buddies** | Friends by code: send "good vibes". **Goal Buddies**: a daily goal shared between two, with an alert when the other completes it. **Accountability Buddies**: one-way sharing |
| **HabitShare** | **Per-habit, per-person privacy** | Everything is private by default; you choose which habit each friend sees. Messages with GIFs. Seems barely maintained since 2023 |
| **Habitify** | Challenges | Challenges with friends or open ones, monthly ranking, compared heatmaps, family plan |
| **Strides** | Shared goals | Share or collaborate on a goal, only among iCloud users |
| **Streaks** | Shared tasks | Via iCloud: the other person sees your progress and stats |
| **Atoms** | Accountability partner | By invitation. **Perceived as empty**: the partner can't do anything |
| **Fabulous** | Community | *Circles* (forums by topic), live community challenges |
| **Productive** | Global challenges | Compete against users worldwide, with no penalty |
| **Loop, Way of Life, HabitNow, TickTick, Routinery** | Nothing or just sharing an image | Routinery: sharing lists, *Explore* |

## Market patterns

- There are four levels of pressure, from least to most:
  1. Sharing or viewing.
  2. Mutual buddy (Finch Goal Buddies).
  3. Social consequence (Habitica: you damage the party).
  4. **Financial consequence** (stickK, Beeminder).
- Money apps have known problems:
  - A **perverse incentive** (the company wins if you fail).
  - Cheating to avoid paying.
  - Charges that are hard to cancel.
  - Regulation (stickK: over 18 and a stake below 10% of income).
- **Per-habit privacy** (HabitShare) is key: nobody shares all their habits.
- A partner unable to act (Atoms) adds nothing.

## Gaps and opportunities

- A **habit contract without money**: written commitment + consequence chosen by the user + a witness who **gets an alert if you miss twice in a row** (and not if you miss once).
- A partner with **real actions**: see your status, get failure alerts, send encouragement or a "nudge" on the recovery day.
- Per-habit, per-person privacy from the start.
- Use the social dimension as "culture" (2nd law): **groups of people with the same identity** ("beginner runners").
