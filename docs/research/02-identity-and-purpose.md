# 02 · Identity and purpose

**Principles from the book it touches:** identity-based habits (outcomes → processes → identity) and "every action is a vote for the person you want to be".

## How each app handles it

| App | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Atoms** | **The only one that puts it at the center.** Each habit is mandatorily linked to an identity ("to become a mindful person") and an outcome. Each check-in is presented as "a vote". It has "reflections" to connect habit and identity, although reviews consider them disconnected and unguided. |
| **Fabulous** | Narrative approach: each Journey is "a chapter of your story", with personalized letters. In onboarding you commit to 3 morning goals. There's no identity field. |
| **Finch** | The emotional bond is with the pet ("the stronger your habits, the stronger the bond"). No identity of your own. |
| **Habitica** | The "identity" is the RPG avatar and its class. |
| **Productive** | *Programs* start by choosing a goal. |
| **Habitify** | *Areas* (life categories) for grouping. No "why". |
| **Loop** | *Question* field and notes: the closest thing to a "why". |
| **Strides** | Notes per goal. |
| **The rest** (Streaks, Way of Life, HabitNow, Beeminder, stickK, TickTick, Routinery, HabitShare) | Don't have it. |

## Market patterns

- Outside Atoms, **no app models identity**. At most they group by "life areas".
- Atoms shows it can be done and also where it fails: the identity is asked for when creating the habit and then **never worked on**. There's no review or vote count per identity.

## Gaps and opportunities

- **Identities as a first-class entity.** An identity groups several habits.
- **Vote counter per identity**: a visible accumulated total ("142 votes for *I'm a person who takes care of myself*"). Helps get through the plateau of latent potential.
- **Periodic identity review** (the book's integrity review): is this identity still yours? Do your habits reflect it?
- Initial **habit scorecard**: list current habits and mark them as +, − or = with respect to the identity.
