## Market patterns

- **The most common complaints are about pricing**:
  - Very low free limit (Atoms with 1 habit, Habitify with 3).
  - Paywall in onboarding.
  - Price increases.
  - No refunds.
- **One-time purchase** (Streaks, HabitNow, Way of Life) is what users like most, although it leaves little margin to maintain the app.
- The "win when the user fails" model (Beeminder, stickK) **breeds distrust** because of the perverse incentive.
- Finch shows that a generous free core, monetized through cosmetics or "support", can work at large scale.
