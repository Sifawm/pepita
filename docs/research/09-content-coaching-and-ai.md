## Market patterns

- Paid content works as a hook (Atoms, Fabulous, Productive), but **the common complaint is that it isn't connected to what you do**.
- AI in 2026 is used for three things:
  1. **Creating habits from text** (Magic Fill, Ruri).
  2. **Analyzing your data** (Habi AI).
  3. **MCP integration** with external assistants (Habitify, Routinery).
- Nobody uses AI to **diagnose why a habit fails according to the 4 laws**.

## Gaps and opportunities

- **Contextual content**: if a habit fails, show the lesson or diagnosis for the corresponding law ("Is it too hard? Switch to the 2-minute version").
- An **AI 4-laws coach**: when creating a habit, help write the implementation intention, the stacking, the minimum version, the environment and the temptation bundling. On failure, run the diagnosis.
- **Watch out for copyright**: the book's text can't be reproduced. Content must be original, inspired by the ideas (ideas aren't copyrighted; the "Atomic Habits" brand is trademarked).
